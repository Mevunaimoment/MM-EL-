24K GOLD PYRAMID & CRIMSON SILK SCREENSAVER (LENOVO IDEAPAD EDITION)
====================================================================

1. ONE-CLICK FULLSCREEN LAUNCH (NO BROWSER BARS):
   - Extract all files in this ZIP into a permanent folder (e.g., C:\Screensavers\24KIntro\).
   - Double-click "Launch-24K-Screensaver.bat" to open immediately in borderless kiosk fullscreen.
   - Press Alt + F4 anytime to exit fullscreen mode.

2. PURE ARTWORK MODE:
   - Move your mouse to reveal the bottom bar and click "Pure Artwork: Off" to hide the corner clock and title text completely.
   - Your choice is saved automatically.

3. AUTOMATIC WINDOWS IDLE SCREENSAVER:
   - Install WebPageScreensaver (WebPageScreensaver.scr).
   - Open Windows "Change screen saver" settings -> click "Settings..." -> enter:
     file:///C:/Screensavers/24KIntro/index.html
